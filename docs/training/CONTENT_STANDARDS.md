# Content standards

The [owner policy](OWNER_POLICY.md) is the full business authority. These standards apply it to each production package; they do not invent new company rules.

## Required lesson package

Every lesson needs measurable objectives and applicable skills; a complete approximately 15-minute narration; scene timestamps; on-screen text; graphics/movement directions; worked conversations; a practice decision; a quiz with explanations; a practical scenario/rubric; transcript; timed captions; printable job aid; publication/version metadata; and a reviewed final media manifest.

Teach the reason, show a bad example, explain the mistake, show a good example, ask for a decision, let the employee practice, give specific feedback, then require a second attempt. Do not pad a short outline with idle loops. The finished video target is 900 seconds; learner pauses and practice extend the session beyond that.

## Company facts versus coaching

Use **OWNER APPROVED** for policy confirmed in the owner brief. Use **PROPOSED** for instructional defaults, assessment thresholds and production choices awaiting review. Use **TECHNICAL LIMITATION** for behavior that is not actually available. Use **FUTURE FEATURE** for later phases.

The purpose of an SDR conversation is a useful qualified next step. A Complimentary Growth Assessment is a 15–20 minute video meeting with a human AI Specialist or designated leadership. Guru is the AI trainer. Do not conflate them.

Teach Problem → Discovery → Impact → Relevant Solution → Next Step. Explore the customer's answer before naming a solution. An existing receptionist can indicate overflow, after-hours or follow-up opportunities; never make firing staff the default pitch.

Owner amendment, 2026-09-27: pricing is custom to the build identified as best for the business. Only qualified human AI Specialists discuss pricing, only during a Growth Assessment. SDRs and Guru do not give starting prices, ranges, estimates, package figures, quotes or discounts. Acknowledge the question, explain why understanding the business comes first, and offer the assessment. Old starting-price guidance is superseded. No integration or delivery guarantee.

## Evidence and practice

Fictional examples must be labeled. Keep fact, hypothesis and agreed next step separate. Invented practice businesses cannot become claimed customer successes. Use real screen captures of synthetic CRM data for walkthroughs. A saved lead or assessment request is not proof of an invitation or a booked calendar time.

Listening exercises must change direction when the prospect's answer does. Include no-fit, busy, skeptical, gatekeeper, price-question, real objection, buying-signal and DNC outcomes. Success is not always a meeting. Explain why the strongest quiz answer beats each tempting mistake. Assess demonstrated conduct; do not infer personality, emotion or permanent employee potential.

Use publicly verifiable sources and current, jurisdiction-specific review before regulated industry lessons. Do not turn cannabis, healthcare, insurance or legal training into medical/legal advice. Do not imply competitor shortcomings without evidence.

## Visual and access standards

Use the established graphite, purple and gold palette. Guru's canonical helmet is `static/img/guru-helmet.jpg`: sealed lower face, no mouth/lips/teeth, narrow violet speech light. Frame the torso and hands with room to gesture. Reject visor deformation and forehead crops. Captions must stay readable on a phone; CRM steps may recommend desktop viewing. Provide keyboard controls, transcripts and printable job aids.

The first module's VTT is a timing draft, not finished audio alignment. Every final caption file must be checked against the approved narration. Owner review of a written package does not imply approval of the voice, pilot, full video or publication.
