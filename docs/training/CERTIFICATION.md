# Certification and readiness

## Owner policy and proposed defaults

Manager approval is mandatory. The initial configurable thresholds are **PROPOSED**: quiz score ≥80%, role-play score ≥8/10 with no critical failure, and ≥90% distinct video coverage. The owner supplied 80% and 8/10 as suggestions; 90% coverage is an implementation default. Edit Track settings in content administration. Historical quiz attempts retain their original passing threshold; current certification checks use the currently configured thresholds.

The four phases are Training, Shadowing, Supervised calling, Certified/independent. Managers may authorize supervised practice before every video is complete. Updating this Academy phase does not change platform security permissions or grant commercial authority.

## Server-enforced prerequisites

1. Every required module has an eligible published version.
2. The employee completes the configured video coverage for that version/current retraining cycle.
3. Its quiz has a passing attempt at the current threshold.
4. Every required scenario has a manager-reviewed passing attempt with no recorded critical failure.
5. A final capstone scenario has manager-reviewed passing evidence.
6. A current manager practical review confirms the mock/supervised call and CRM documentation exercise.
7. A manager approves certification with a reason. The application saves the requirements snapshot and reviewer.

A track with no required modules, an unproduced lesson or a retired required version cannot be certified. Watching alone, client-submitted scores and Guru advisory feedback cannot award readiness. Module 1 practice is not the final Core capstone; that belongs to Module 16 and is currently in production.

Critical failures include lying, invented claims/pricing/integrations, ROI guarantees, abusive behavior, ignoring DNC, fabricated CRM notes or deliberately unqualified meetings. Managers assess the evidence; the AI does not silently make an employment decision.

## Versioning and retraining

Approved/published/retired content is immutable through normal app/admin edits. Create a draft revision, update it, obtain owner review, review media/captions and publish through the owner workflow. Publishing does not migrate existing assigned versions or erase their evidence. Retire the previous published version explicitly. Managers define who must train on the replacement.

Assign retraining to a published version with a note and optional deadline. The old assignment becomes inactive, a new watch cycle starts, fresh quiz/practice evidence is required, and existing certification is revoked. A new practical review after retraining is also required before recertification. Earlier watch cycles, quiz attempts, feedback, reviews and certification snapshots remain stored.

The manager's “Revoke” action records a reason, preserves the award and moves the track to supervised status. An independent phase cannot be set through the ordinary phase form. No automated job silently promotes an employee.

## What the MVP does not decide

Completing an advanced or industry track does not authorize quotes, discounts, legal/medical advice or system access. Industry prerequisites, approval delegations, reminders and certifications with expiry dates are later configuration/workflow work. No commission, quota or universal outreach cadence is inferred from certification.
