# Erykha's automotive field guide

The printable, internal one-page version is `/demo/automotive/guide/`, linked from Demo controls.

Ask for the BDC Director, Internet Director, GSM, GM or Dealer Principal.

“I actually spent about 14 years in automotive and BDC, so there's a specific reason I'm here...” Explain briefly that AI Business Gurus helps identify where AI could support their actual process.

Ask: What CRM? Centralized BDC or floor-managed? Fresh lead response time? After-hours handling? Follow-up duration? Old-database mining? Appointment confirmations and show rates? Separate service BDC?

| Pain | Demonstration |
| --- | --- |
| Slow response | Fresh internet lead |
| After-hours gaps | 11:47 PM scenario |
| Untouched old database | Reactivation |
| Missed calls | Missed-call scenario; phone is a future channel concept |
| Service workload | Service scheduling |
| Repetitive inventory questions | Vehicle shopping / inventory |
| Appointment friction | Test drive |

“Honestly, it's easier if I show you. Talk to it like you'd talk to one of your BDC reps.” Let the prospect drive the conversation. Use fictional details. Show a search/comparison, trade and demo appointment only as relevant.

Tap **Show dealership view → Enter the dealership CRM**. Show the customer, appointment, trade, transcript, summary and recommended follow-up. “This is what your team gets.” Demonstrate a staff assignment/note if useful. For the financing flow, provide a fictional phone/email, say you would like to explore dealership financing, and accept Axel’s offer to open the demo application. Review a sample individual or business profile, submit it, and show its vehicle, contact details and finance-team assignment in the CRM. No real financial information is needed.

“This same intelligence can live on your website, phones, SMS workflows and other customer touchpoints depending on your systems and implementation.” Do not claim a named dealer CRM or phone system is connected in this demonstration.

“Would it be worth spending 15 or 20 minutes looking at how this could fit into your actual BDC?” Offer a complimentary Growth Assessment with an AI Specialist. Pricing is custom to the identified build; only AI Specialists discuss pricing during the assessment. No guaranteed results or integrations.

Share the attributed QR/link, report any issue in Demo controls, and reset for the next prospect. Test internet/microphone before each visit. Keep a hotspot available; this is not an offline AI app.
