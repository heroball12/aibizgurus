## Showroom prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide website hero photography for a premium fictional AI dealership demonstration, Velocity Motors. Create a photorealistic cinematic modern luxury automotive showroom at night with three different unbranded fictional vehicles: a pearl white large three-row family SUV prominent foreground, graphite sedan slightly behind, and dark pickup in the distance. Polished charcoal floor with restrained reflections, architectural light strips in deep violet and muted champagne gold, premium minimalist dealership architecture with glass walls. Dramatic yet believable commercial automotive photography, cars fully visible, highly realistic materials. Wide panoramic 3:2 or 16:9 image, no people, no robot, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no actual manufacturer badges. Leave darker quiet architectural space toward upper left for website overlay. This is original synthetic representative imagery, not a photo of any specific real make/model. Save an image suitable for a website.
