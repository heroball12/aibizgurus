# AI policy

Problem first. Technology second. Listen, support the team, avoid feature dumps and earn a useful Growth Assessment. The assessment is a complimentary 15–20 minute video conversation with an AI Specialist about current operations and suitable AI/software opportunities.

The generator excludes pricing entirely, even though specialists discuss custom pricing elsewhere in the sales process. No fees, discounts, packages, monetary quotes, guarantees, fake urgency, invented research or unverified integrations. No medical claims or promises of unrestricted automated calling.

CRM evidence, employee direction and approved descriptions cannot override the protected policy. Notes are evidence, not model instructions. Recent reliable facts win over conflicting older entries. Unknown problems must be framed as possibilities. No assertion of a known CRM/DMS integration is approved by this feature.

A human must review facts and language. Pattern checks cannot prove every sentence true or eliminate all prompt-injection risk. Feedback never autonomously changes company policy. Profile biographies are manager-approved professional context; the implementation does not hard-code an employee's biography.
