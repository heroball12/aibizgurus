# Approved service catalog

Seventeen database-backed options are seeded from `crm/email_generator/defaults.py`: outbound calling, receptionist/intake, lead response, appointments, customer service, reactivation, operations, CRM workflows, website concierge, internal knowledge, AI Budtender, automotive/BDC, custom software, web/mobile apps, websites, business management software and custom AI.

Managers use Sales → Workspace → Email copilot settings to rename, reorder, enable/disable and edit descriptions. Stable IDs are protected. Seeding never overwrites edits.

General introduction excludes specific services. Selected services requires at least one enabled category. Multiple services should form one coherent narrative, prioritized by explicit pain, employee direction, industry, conversation and finally selection order. Suggestions are deterministic, explained and never automatically checked.
