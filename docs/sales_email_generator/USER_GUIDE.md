# Employee guide

1. Open a lead you are assigned and save your latest call notes.
2. Click **Generate email**. Choose the actual conversation type.
3. Choose General introduction or one or more relevant services. Adjust length, tone, links or your direction if useful.
4. Generate. Choose a subject and edit the email and signature. Check every name, fact and link.
5. **Copy subject** and **Copy email** paste into Gmail's separate fields. **Copy all** gives the subject first, then a blank line and the complete email; place the first line in Gmail's Subject field.
6. **Save to CRM** retains an explicitly saved draft. Generation and copy alone never count as sent.
7. After sending it yourself, choose **I sent it · mark as sent** and confirm. CRM records this as self-reported email activity.

Edits autosave and have a per-user/per-lead tab recovery copy. Conflicting tab revisions cannot silently overwrite each other. Regeneration creates a new version and can use your edited draft. History retains earlier saved versions. Start over keeps history; discard marks a draft discarded. A new assignee can review prior history but cannot edit or mark another employee's draft sent.

No email address is required to write a draft. Review the displayed recipient in Gmail yourself. This phase does not connect Gmail, open a connected inbox, send SMS or automatically deliver email.
