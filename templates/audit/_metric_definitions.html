How these metrics are counted

Calls / updates: one saved manual lead change or note per lead per request, credited to the person making it. Saving unchanged fields, importing files, viewing pages and automatic research do not add calls. This is your CRM activity measure; it does not prove a phone call was connected.

Assessments: a confirmed date/time and a booking status are required. Bookings, completed assessments, proposals and wins count distinct leads with that outcome during the selected period. Repeated saves do not add bookings. Booking rate is booked leads ÷ leads worked in the period; clocked hours include the portion of each shift inside the selected dates.

Current pipeline: assigned, booked now, due, overdue, warm/hot, archived and needs-review columns show the employee’s current inventory, independent of the date filter. Activity remains credited to its author after reassignment or lead deletion. Training counts completed videos, not certifications.
