Runway processing and recordings

By checking the agreement box and starting a conversation, you consent to a live AI call processed by Runway. Your typed messages and any microphone audio you choose to share are sent to Runway to provide the conversation. Audio, conversation recordings and transcripts may be retained by Runway and accessible to AI Business Gurus through the provider’s tools.

The website does not separately save call audio, video or transcripts. Your camera stays off. Microphone access requires your browser’s permission; you can type instead, mute your microphone, or end the call at any time. Ending a call stops further input but does not delete information already processed.
