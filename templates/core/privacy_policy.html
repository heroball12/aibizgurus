AI Business Gurus ("we," "us," or "our") provides AI employees, website chatbots, voice AI, automation, and related business services. This policy explains how we handle personal information through aibiz.guru, our demonstrations, account workspaces, and our communications with you.

When a business uses our services to process its own customers' information, that business may control how the information is used. Its privacy notice and our agreement with that business also apply. Workplace and personnel records may be subject to separate employee notices.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, telephone number, business name, industry, website, appointment preferences, messages, and other details you submit through forms, accounts, consultations, or support requests.
  • Account and service information: account identifiers, authentication information, business settings, uploaded documents, CRM records, service activity, and billing or subscription references. Payment providers process payment details when you use their checkout.
  • Conversations and AI interactions: messages, conversation summaries, and information you choose to share with an assistant. If you enable a microphone, audio is sent to the provider supporting that feature. Different assistants retain different information, as explained below and in the feature's notice.
  • Technical and activity information: IP address, browser and device information, approximate location inferred from IP address, pages visited, referring pages, timestamps, cookies, session identifiers, and security or diagnostic logs.
  • Business contact information from other sources: professional contact and company details from public listings, business contacts, customer-provided records, and providers such as ZoomInfo. We may combine these details with existing CRM records for business development and relationship management.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card numbers, government identification numbers, medical details, or other sensitive information in chat, demonstrations, or general inquiry forms.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and administer our services; answer inquiries; schedule assessments and appointments; configure and support business workflows; maintain accounts and CRM records; process billing; communicate about services; understand website use; improve features; prevent misuse; resolve disputes; and meet legal obligations. We may use professional contact information to tell businesses about relevant services, subject to applicable communication rules and your choices.

3. AI conversations, voice, and demonstrations

Our AI features use providers such as OpenAI and Runway. The provider supporting a feature receives the messages, context, or audio needed to operate it. AI responses can contain errors, and you should avoid sharing information you are not authorized to disclose.

Website chatbot conversations can be saved with contact details and made available to the business operating that chatbot and its authorized staff. Interactive demonstrations can also store conversation text and demo activity. Use fictional details in demonstrations.

The live Guru video assistant uses Runway. The website does not separately save that assistant's call audio, video, or transcripts, but Runway may retain recordings or transcripts and make them available to us through its tools. The website can retain call identifiers, status, and timing. Optional introductions and short visit summaries may be kept in your website session for up to two hours. The live assistant does not turn on your camera. Microphone use requires browser permission, and you can type instead or end the call.

Read the live assistant terms and recording notice before starting a call. Ending a session stops further input; it does not automatically delete information already processed by us or a provider.

4. Cookies, visitor identification, and your choices

We use cookies and similar storage for sign-in, security, sessions, and feature preferences. Blocking these technologies can prevent parts of the site from working.

On selected public pages, we use ZoomInfo's visitor-identification technology to understand visits and identify potential business interest. ZoomInfo may collect identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and browsing activity and associate them with company or professional profiles. Its processing may include activity across websites. Depending on the law and the provider's use of the information, this disclosure may be considered a sale or sharing of personal information.

We suppress the ZoomInfo website tag when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal or a Do Not Track signal. This choice affects future tag loading in that browser; it does not delete earlier information or opt you out of ZoomInfo's separate business-contact database. You can also use browser controls to block third-party scripts and cookies.

To request an opt-out of sale or sharing, or ask about information we hold, email support@aibiz.guru. For information held independently by ZoomInfo, see its privacy policy and privacy request center.

5. When we share information

  • Service providers: hosting, database and storage providers, AI providers, communications and scheduling services, payment processors, and support providers that help operate the services. Depending on the feature, these include Render, OpenAI, Runway, Twilio, Calendly, and Stripe.
  • Authorized businesses and staff: the business responsible for a chatbot or account, and personnel who need access to handle inquiries, provide support, or manage services.
  • Business intelligence providers: ZoomInfo as described above. Its independent processing is governed by its own privacy notice.
  • Legal and business needs: when required by law, to protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable protections.
  • At your direction: when you ask us to share information or authorize an integration.

6. Email and text messages

You can ask us to stop marketing communications by contacting support or using an unsubscribe option in a message. We may still send necessary account, service, or security communications. If you opt in to a text-message program, its sign-up notice describes the messages and how to stop them. Mobile numbers and text-message opt-in information are not shared with third parties for their own marketing; providers may process them to deliver and support the messaging service.

7. Retention and security

Retention depends on the type of information and why it was collected. We consider active service needs, relationship history, contractual requirements, security, disputes, and legal recordkeeping obligations. Information may remain in backups or provider systems for additional periods. A session expiry or the end of a demo does not necessarily erase stored records.

We use safeguards such as access controls and encrypted connections to protect information. No system or transmission method is completely secure. Contact us if you believe an account or information has been compromised.

8. Access, correction, deletion, and other rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to know about or access personal information, obtain a copy, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising, restrict certain processing, or appeal a privacy decision. California residents may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act when it applies. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Send requests to support@aibiz.guru or call {{ company.phone }}. Describe your request and the email address or business connected with your interaction. We may need to verify your identity or an authorized agent's authority, and some information may need to be retained where permitted or required by law. If we process information on another business's behalf, we may direct the request to that business. You can ask us to review a decision by replying to our response.

9. Children and international visitors

Our services are intended for business use and are not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address it. We operate in the United States, and our providers may process information in other countries whose privacy laws differ from those where you live.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. We will post the revised policy with an updated date and provide additional notice when required. Contact AI Business Gurus at support@aibiz.guru with privacy questions or requests.
